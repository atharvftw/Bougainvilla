## 1 · THE STRATEGIC READ ON SEPTEMBER

September is not one month. It is three windows with completely different demand,
and the August calendar structure (offer carousel at month end) would put the
hardest-working creative in the weakest window.

| Window | Dates | Demand | What the content does |
|---|---|---|---|

| C · Pitru Paksha | Sep 25-30 | LOWEST | Build the asset, pre-sell October. |

"City festival — Mumbai and Thane crowds stay in town. Not a villa weekend."

week to Week 1 (Sep 2).** In August it closed the month because Aug 31 was the
target day. In September the back half is dead, so the offer must land early.

## 3 · MONTH RULES — SEPTEMBER (deltas from August in bold)

Carried forward unchanged:
- No rupee figures in the feed. Ever.
- No 'limited time', no countdowns, no false scarcity.
- Every offer ends in WhatsApp, never link in bio.
- WhatsApp icon + number on every static: bottom third, brand brown, not green.
- Every carousel ends on a full WhatsApp slide, not a corner overlay.
- 4:5 / 1080×1350. Centre-square grid-crop safe zone. One typeface. No emoji,
  no shadows, no dark scrims, no magenta.

New for September:
- **Shravan has ended — non-veg and bar imagery are technically permitted again
  from Sep 1. Do not use them anyway from Sep 14 onward:** a large share of this
  audience is vegetarian through Ganpati, and strictly so through Pitru Paksha.
  Safe window for any food/bar imagery is **Sep 1–13 only.**
- **Prices appear on the OFFERS highlight frames and nowhere else.**
- **No offer content at all Sep 14–24 (Ganpati) and Sep 26–30 (Pitru Paksha).**
  Offer creative runs Sep 1–13 and stops.
- **No Ganpati murti imagery, no idol photography, no visarjan footage.**
  A greeting, a diya, a marigold detail, the villa. Nothing devotional used
  as a marketing frame.
- **Font reference supplied** (your `Dblrpj-E_Lf` link). Designer picks ONE
  typeface from it. The one-typeface rule from August still holds absolutely.
- **The lake is now a headline asset.** It did not appear anywhere in August.
  It gets a static, a reel beat, a story, and a slot in three highlights.
